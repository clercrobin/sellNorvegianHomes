## 1. Target Audience & Personas

### 🎯 Primary Persona 1: "Éco-Conscient Urbain"

**Demographics:**
- Age: 35-50 years old
- Location: Greater Paris, Lyon, Toulouse (looking to move to peri-urban/countryside)
- Income: €60,000-€100,000/year household
- Education: University degree
- Family: Couple with 1-2 children

**Psychographics:**
- Values sustainability and eco-responsibility
- Works remotely or hybrid (at least 2 days/week from home)
- Tired of city life, seeking nature and space
- Interested in Scandinavian lifestyle and design
- Active on Instagram, Pinterest, Facebook groups
- Reads blogs about sustainable living

**Pain Points:**
- Traditional construction takes too long (12-18 months)
- Uncertain costs and hidden fees
- Poor energy efficiency in older homes
- Complex administrative processes
- Fear of poor quality from unknown builders

**Motivations:**
- Lower energy bills
- Faster move-in timeline
- Predictable budget
- Healthy living environment
- Beautiful design that impresses guests

**Where to find them:**
- Facebook: Groups on construction, eco-living, rural living
- Instagram: Following #maisonnature #constructionbois #viealacompagne
- Forums: Forumconstruire.com, aufeminin.com (maison section)
- Events: Salons de l'habitat, eco-construction fairs

---

### 🎯 Primary Persona 2: "Retraité Actif"

**Demographics:**
- Age: 55-70 years old
- Location: Across France (downsizing from city or larger home)
- Income: €50,000-€80,000/year (pensions + savings)
- Family: Couple, children grown and independent

**Psychographics:**
- Planning retirement or recently retired
- Wants a comfortable, low-maintenance home
- Health-conscious, values comfort and accessibility
- Has time to research thoroughly before deciding
- Prefers phone calls over online forms

**Pain Points:**
- Don't want stairs (prefer plain-pied models)
- Concerned about future resale value
- Worried about construction quality and guarantees
- Need a trusted partner for the entire process
- Limited technical knowledge about construction

**Motivations:**
- One-floor living for aging in place
- Low heating costs on fixed income
- Minimal maintenance required
- Quick construction (don't want to wait years)
- Peaceful retirement in nature

**Where to find them:**
- Facebook groups: Retraite, vie senior, jardinage
- Magazines: Notre Temps, Pleine Vie, Rustica
- Local events: Retirement planning seminars
- Notaire offices: People selling larger homes

---

### 🎯 Secondary Persona 3: "Investisseur Location Saisonnière"

**Demographics:**
- Age: 30-55 years old
- Location: Urban centers (Paris, Bordeaux, Lyon)
- Income: €80,000-€150,000/year
- Occupation: Entrepreneurs, professionals, investors

**Psychographics:**
- Looking for rental investment opportunities
- Interested in Airbnb / gîtes in tourist areas
- Values ROI and rental yield
- Wants turnkey solutions
- Active on LinkedIn and investment forums

**Pain Points:**
- Complex traditional construction for secondary properties
- Need fast construction to start earning rental income
- Uncertainty about regulations and permits
- Want unique properties that stand out on Airbnb

**Motivations:**
- Fast ROI (construction in 4-6 months)
- Attractive design for high nightly rates
- Low energy costs = higher profit margins
- Scandinavian aesthetic appeals to tourists
- Potential for premium pricing

**Where to find them:**
- LinkedIn: Real estate investment groups
- Forums: Investissement locatif, Airbnb hosts
- Facebook: Groupes investissement immobilier
- Podcasts: Real estate investment shows
- Networking: Real estate meetups, chambers of commerce

## 2. AI Text Content Prompts

### Blog Article Prompts (for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)

#### Article 1: Comparative Guide
```
Prompt:
Write a comprehensive 1500-word blog article in French titled "Maison Préfabriquée Norvégienne vs Construction Traditionnelle : Le Guide Complet 2025".

Include:
- Opening hook about rising construction costs and delays in France
- Comparison table: timeline, costs, energy efficiency, quality
- 5 key advantages of prefab Norwegian houses for French climate
- Real cost breakdown example (foundation to keys)
- Address common concerns (durability, resale value, financing)
- Environmental benefits (carbon footprint, sustainable materials)
- Call-to-action to download a free comparison PDF

Tone: Professional yet warm, educational, reassuring
Target: French homebuyers researching construction options
SEO keywords: maison préfabriquée norvégienne, construction rapide, maison bois, maison passive
```

#### Article 2: Financing Guide
```
Prompt:
Write a detailed 1200-word article in French: "Comment Financer Votre Maison Préfabriquée Norvégienne : Prêts, Aides et Subventions 2025"

Cover:
- Bank loan requirements and process for prefab homes
- PTZ (Prêt à Taux Zéro) eligibility
- MaPrimeRénov' and eco-construction grants
- Regional aid programs (examples from 3 French regions)
- Tax benefits for energy-efficient homes
- Step-by-step financing timeline
- Tips for negotiating with banks
- Common mistakes to avoid
- Real example: €250,000 home financing breakdown

Include expert quotes from a fictional mortgage broker.
Tone: Practical, reassuring, detailed
CTA: "Speak with our financing partner"
```

#### Article 3: Process Walkthrough
```
Prompt:
Create a 1000-word guide in French: "De la Première Visite à la Remise des Clés : 6 Mois pour Construire Votre Maison Norvégienne"

- Month 1: First meeting, terrain analysis, model selection
- Month 2: Permit application, financing finalization
- Month 3-4: Factory production in Norway (with photos description)
- Month 5: Transport and on-site assembly
- Month 6: Finishes and handover

For each phase:
- What happens
- What you need to do
- Common questions
- Timeline checkpoints

Include personal story of fictional client "Sophie & Marc from Annecy"
Tone: Storytelling, exciting, detailed
CTA: "Start your journey today"

#### Article 4: Design Inspiration
```

Write an inspiring 1000-word article in French: "15 Idées d'Aménagement Intérieur pour Votre Maison Scandinave en France"
